# Phase 03: Resources

Curated resources to master LangGraph, state machines, and multi-turn agentic architectures.

### Official Documentation
1. **[LangGraph Quickstart Guide](https://langchain-ai.github.io/langgraph/tutorials/introduction/)** - The absolute best place to start. This tutorial walks you through building a chatbot, adding tools, and adding memory step-by-step.
2. **[LangGraph Conceptual Guides](https://langchain-ai.github.io/langgraph/concepts/)** - Deep dives into how State, Nodes, Edges, and Checkpointing actually work under the hood. Crucial reading for backend engineers.
3. **[LangGraph: Time Travel](https://langchain-ai.github.io/langgraph/how-tos/time-travel/)** - Documentation on how to use checkpointers to rewind state, modify it, and resume execution.

### Articles & Tutorials
4. **[DeepLearning.AI: AI Agents in LangGraph (Free Course)](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/ai-agents-in-langgraph/)** - Taught by Harrison Chase (creator of LangChain). A 1-hour video course that visually explains graph architectures perfectly.
5. **[Why LangGraph? (Over standard LangChain)](https://blog.langchain.dev/langgraph/)** - The official launch blog post explaining why directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) and state machines are the correct abstraction for agentic systems.

### Tools & Repositories
6. **[LangSmith](https://www.langchain.com/langsmith)** - While covered more in Phase 9, setting up LangSmith (free tier) now is highly recommended. It visualizes your LangGraph execution traces in a web UI, making debugging 100x easier.
7. **[LangGraph Studio](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langgraph-studio)** - A visual IDE for LangGraph (currently available for Mac/Cloud) that lets you visually debug and interact with your compiled graphs.
